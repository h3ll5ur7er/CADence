## Where CADence runs

- **On the web** at https://h3ll5ur7er.github.io/CADence/. Projects stay in your browser. After you have modelled a part once, CADence also works without an internet connection; your browser may offer to install it as an app (*Install CADence* in the address bar or the browser menu). When a new version is ready, a notice in the bottom right corner offers to reload.
- **On the desktop** as the CADence app for Windows, with projects as folders on your computer. Install it from the [releases](https://github.com/h3ll5ur7er/CADence/releases) (the `.exe` installs for you alone, without administrator rights; the `.msi` for everyone). At start, the app looks for a newer release; a notice in the bottom right corner offers to install it and restart.

**About CADence** (the ⓘ button on the home page and in the editor) shows the version, links to the source code and this guide, and the libraries and fonts CADence is built on, with their licences and full licence texts.

Wherever it runs, CADence records versions of your projects as you work and can sync them through a git repository ([[User-guide/Projects-and-versions]]). Scripts, the command line and AI agents can model and export too ([[User-guide/Automation]]).
