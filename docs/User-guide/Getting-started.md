# Getting started

The first steps in CADence: a short tour that models a first part with you, and samples to look around in.

[TOC]

## The tour

The first time CADence starts on a computer or a tablet, a card in the bottom right corner offers a tour of a first part (not on phones, where CADence is a viewer). **Take the tour**, or **Skip** it; **Take the tour** on the home page (and *Take the tour* in the command palette, **Ctrl+K**) starts it again whenever you want.

The tour points at one button at a time with a ring and moves on when you have pressed it:

1. **New project**, an empty one.
2. **New sketch** on the XY plane (top).
3. A **rectangle**: the Rectangle tool, then two corners in the view; dimensions set its size. Press **Next** when it is drawn.
4. **Finish sketch**: the closed rectangle is a profile.
5. **Extrude** the profile, by 6 mm for example, and press OK: the part has a body.
6. **Export** it for a slicer (STL, 3MF), another CAD program (STEP), or as a viewer to share.

**Skip tour** ends it at any step. The card never covers the page: do each step in the app itself. If you start the tour in a project that is already open, it begins at the sketch.

## Samples

The **Samples** at the bottom of the home page are finished projects: *Box with lid* (two parts and an assembly), *Wall bracket* and *Enclosure*. A click makes a project of your own from one, named after it, and opens it; the sample itself stays as it is for the next time. Their sizes are project parameters: change them in the **Parameters** panel and watch every part follow ([[User-guide/Part-modeling#parameters]]).

## Help in the app

The **?** beside a panel's title (Measure, Parameters, a feature, a joint, a motion study …) or in a dialog's heading opens its page of this guide in your browser. **?** on the keyboard lists the shortcuts of your control style ([[User-guide/Viewing-and-controls#keyboard-shortcuts]]).
