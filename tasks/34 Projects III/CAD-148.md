Deleted projects and documents go to a trash first and can be restored for a while (30 days), on the web and on the desktop; agents can list and restore them too.

- [ ] Deleting moves a project or document to the trash (its versions with it)
- [ ] The home page lists the trash; restore or delete for good
- [ ] Emptied after 30 days, or by hand
- [ ] MCP: `delete_*` says it went to the trash; list and restore it
- [ ] Test: a deleted document comes back with its history
