title: Standard parts as assembly instances
type: feature
status: in-progress
priority: medium
---

Screws, nuts, washers, bearings and the other catalogue parts can be placed in assemblies as instances from a shared standard library ([[CAD-151]]), not only modelled into a part; they appear in the bill of materials by size.

- [ ] A built-in standard library of the catalogue parts, in every size
- [ ] Insert into an assembly with a joint on a hole (screw into a hole, nut on a screw)
- [ ] The bill of materials lists them by designation (ISO 4762 M3 × 12)
- [ ] Test: an assembly with screws from the library, its bill of materials
