Screws, nuts, washers, bearings and the other catalogue parts can be placed in assemblies as instances from a shared standard library ([[CAD-151]]), not only modelled into a part; they appear in the bill of materials by size.

- [x] A built-in standard library of the catalogue parts, in every size
- [x] Insert into an assembly with a joint on a hole (screw into a hole, nut on a screw)
- [x] The bill of materials lists them by designation (ISO 4762 M3 × 12)
- [x] Test: an assembly with screws from the library, its bill of materials

Done: the built-in standard library ([[src/core/managers/project/standard-library.ts]]): 131 parts, ISO 4762 screws in their stocked lengths, ISO 4032 nuts, ISO 7089 washers, heat-set inserts and ball bearings, each a steel component with a part feature at the origin, named by its designation. The assembly's Insert → From a library… puts a part onto a picked hole edge or round face with a revolute joint, in one change (`insert-instance` with `joint`); the bill of materials lists it by designation. Tests: two M3 × 12 screws in a plate's holes and the bill of materials in [[src/automation/libraries.spec.ts]], the journey in [[e2e/projects-3.spec.ts]] (evidence `phase-34-standard-parts`).
