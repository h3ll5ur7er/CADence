Wrap a sketch around a cylinder or cone, or project it onto a curved face, to emboss or engrave labels, grips and scales.

- [x] Wrap onto cylinders and cones (lengths kept along the surface)
- [x] Project onto any face along a direction
- [x] Emboss and engrave the wrapped profiles by a depth
- [x] Text works the same way
- [x] Test: a scale engraved around a cylinder
