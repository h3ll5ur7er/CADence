Timeline folders inside folders; repair candidates for lost references matched by shape (size and kind of face or edge) as well as distance.

- [x] Folders in folders: create, move, collapse
- [x] Repair candidates ranked by shape and distance
- [x] Test: a lost fillet edge finds the same-size edge, not the nearest

Done in phase 36: folders nest by their items (apart, or one holds the other; `nestFolders` gives each its parent and depth), `move-folder` moves a folder with the folders in it (drag, or Alt+↑/↓ on the folder), closing a folder hides those inside. The Design Manager records each face's and edge's shape when a command names it (`TopologyRef.shape`, Analysis Engine `shapesOf`), and repairs rank candidates by shape, then distance, marking `sameShape` ([[Architecture/Decisions#ADR-55]]). References recorded before this phase have no shape and are ranked by distance as before.
