Ring gears get root fillets like external gears; gears take a profile shift, so gears with few teeth avoid undercut and centre distances can be adjusted.

- [x] Root fillets on ring gears
- [x] Profile shift coefficient on gears (and the mate's centre distance from it)
- [x] Test: a gear of 8 teeth with a shift has no undercut

**Done (phase 37).** Ring gears' tooth spaces get root fillets (`ringFillet` in [[src/core/engines/modeling/gears.ts]]): the largest circle (at most 0.38 m) touching the root circle and the involute beyond the planets' tips; a planet still meshes at (ring − planet) × m / 2. Gears take `shift` (× module): thicker teeth, tips and roots x·m out; the dialog warns below `minimumProfileShift` and gives the centre distance with the mate's shift (`centreDistance` solves the working pressure angle). The unit test rolls the cutting rack along an 8-tooth gear: 0.05 m into the teeth unshifted, no more than the flank fit's tolerance at x = 0.55; a shifted 8-tooth pinion meshes with a 20-tooth gear at 28.98 mm in the kernel. Ring gears take no shift yet, and tips are not shortened for x₁ + x₂ > 0 ([[Architecture/Decisions#ADR-56]]).
