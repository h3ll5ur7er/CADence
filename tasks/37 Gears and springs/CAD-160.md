Bevel gears (straight teeth, a shaft angle, 90° by default) and worms with worm wheels, with where the mate goes, as the spur gear part already says.

- [x] Bevel gear pair: module, teeth, shaft angle, face width
- [x] Worm and worm wheel: module, starts, teeth, lead angle
- [x] Mate positions and motion links (gear ratio) for both
- [x] Test: a bevel pair and a worm drive mesh without interference

**Done (phase 37).** `bevel-gear` parts: the virtual spur gear's teeth laid onto the cone and scaled to its apex ([[src/core/engines/modeling/gears.ts]]); `worm` (threads swept along a helix, [[src/core/engines/modeling/helices.ts]]) and `worm-wheel` (a crossed helical gear at the lead angle) parts. `bevelMesh` and `wormMesh` say where the mate goes, shown in the part dialog; motion links *Bevel gears* and *Worm and wheel* take their ratios from teeth and starts. In the kernel a 20 : 30 bevel pair at 90° overlaps by 0.01 mm³ (12 mm³ at 1° off), a worm and its 30-tooth wheel by less than 0.01 mm³. Not built: throated worm wheels, back cones on bevel gears ([[Architecture/Decisions#ADR-56]]).
