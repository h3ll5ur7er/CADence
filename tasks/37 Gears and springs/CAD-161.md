Helices as paths (sweeps, threads) and spring parts: compression, extension and torsion springs from wire size, pitch, turns and ends (closed, ground).

- [x] Helix as a feature: pitch, turns or height, taper
- [x] Compression, extension and torsion springs as parts
- [x] Spring rate in component properties
- [x] Test: a compression spring of 8 turns, its length and rate

**Done (phase 37).** A `helix` feature (plane, diameter, pitch, turns or height, taper, hand) makes no body and is a sweep path, shown as a curve. `compression-spring` (open, closed or ground ends), `extension-spring` (side loops) and `torsion-spring` (straight legs) parts are each one sweep of a circle along one B-spline centre line ([[src/core/engines/modeling/helices.ts]]). `springProperties` gives the rate (G d⁴ / 8 D³ nₐ, or E d⁴ / 64 D nₐ per radian) and lengths from the material, in the component's properties and `component_properties`. An 8-turn ground spring of ⌀1 steel wire, ⌀10 outside, pitch 3: 20 mm free, 8 mm solid, 2.20 N/mm. Patterns along a helix are refused ([[Architecture/Decisions#ADR-56]]).
