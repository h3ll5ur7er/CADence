Flanges on flanges unfold right in the flat pattern; corners where flanges meet get reliefs (round, square, tear).

- [x] Flanges on flanges in the flat pattern
- [x] Corner reliefs: round, square, tear; size
- [x] Test: a box of four flanges unfolds without overlaps

Notes: the flat pattern keeps a flat panel for the base flange and every straight piece of every flange, and lays each flange flat from the panel its edge lies on ([[Architecture/Decisions#ADR-57]]). The corner relief is a rule of the sheet on the base flange (`cornerRelief`, `reliefSize`); the flat pattern cuts it exactly, the solid gets a straight prism through the sheet and the start of the bends (`f:<feature>:relief:<n>`), and a tear removes nothing.
