- [x] Hems: closed, open, teardrop; gap and length
- [x] Miter flange along a chain of edges from a profile
- [x] Test: a hem and a miter flange unfold

Notes: hems and miter flanges are sections of bends and straight pieces like edge flanges ([[Architecture/Decisions#ADR-57]]). A teardrop bends on until its end is a tenth of the thickness above the sheet, so it never touches the sheet along a line. A miter flange's profile is a sketch of straight lines square to one of its edges (each line a straight piece after a bend by the angle it turns); its pieces are trimmed where the edges meet and their ends cut along the neutral layer in the flat pattern.
