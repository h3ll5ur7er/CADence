Section views along a stepped cutting line (offset) and half sections (half the view cut), with their cutting lines on the parent view.

- [x] Offset section: a stepped cutting line of several segments
- [x] Half section about the view's centre line
- [x] Test: an offset section through two holes not in line

A view's `section` takes `steps` (from `at` mm along the view's right, or up with `along: 'v'`, the plane moves to another `offset`) and `half`. The engine cuts with one box per piece ([[src/core/engines/modeling/drawing-view.ts]]) and leaves out the step's edges; the parent view's cutting line steps with it, thick at each turn; a half section draws its centre line ([[src/core/managers/drawing/layout.ts]]). `sectionPieces` is in the contracts so engine and layout agree. Tests: [[src/core/engines/modeling/drawing-view.spec.ts]], [[src/core/managers/drawing/drawing-manager.spec.ts]], journey [[e2e/drawings-3.spec.ts]]. Only planes across the view: aligned sections are left for later ([[Architecture/Decisions#ADR-58]]).
