Threads drawn as their major and minor diameters (ISO 6410): thin and thick lines, a three-quarter circle in end views; modelled threads drawn the same way instead of their slow helix.

- [x] Threads (markers and modelled) drawn per ISO 6410 in every view
- [x] Thread callouts read from them
- [x] Test: a threaded shaft and hole on a sheet, drawn fast

Component results carry thread markers per body (the Design Manager passes them to `snapshot`; placed assembly copies move them). Views draw thin minor/major lines, the thick end line and the three-quarter circle, seen or hidden with the face; a cut that takes a thread away leaves it out. Modelled threads are drawn from the body with the groove filled (`threadFilled` in [[src/core/engines/modeling/holes.ts]]): a modelled M10 seen along its axis draws in about a second instead of minutes. Threads offer `thread:<feature>` circles for callouts (*M10 thread*, *length 8*; a tapped hole as its hole). Results published before carry no threads until their component changes.
