Views offer the outlines of cones, spheres and free-form faces as anchors, as they do for cylinders, and anchors on the parts of assembly views, not only their outermost points.

- [x] Outlines of cones, spheres and free-form faces as anchors
- [x] Anchors on each part of an assembly view (by instance)
- [x] Test: a cone's outline and an instance's hole dimensioned

Cones give outline lines (sampled: the kernel's cone and torus types are not bound in WebAssembly), spheres a circle `s:<face>`, tori seen along their axis rims `:outer`/`:inner`, other round faces the outermost points of their outline from triangles. `snapshotPlaced` keeps names, so assembly views name every part's anchors `<instance>/<name>`. Hole callouts on assembly parts still give the diameter only (the component's features are not read per instance).
