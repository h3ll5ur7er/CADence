A view drawn from the triangles because the exact projection took too long is projected exactly in the background and replaces the approximate one when done.

- [x] Exact projection goes on in the views worker without a time limit, one view at a time
- [x] The sheet shows the exact view when it is there; the note goes
- [x] Test: a slow view is replaced by its exact one

A second views worker (`exact`: `stoppable` with no limit, stopped after a minute idle) draws the views that ran out of time, one at a time for all drawings of the manager, skipping those no longer shown. Each exact view goes into the drawing's result (also when the drawing was closed) and the sheet is laid out again; meanwhile the note says it is still being drawn. Test in [[src/core/managers/drawing/drawing-manager.spec.ts]]; `stoppable` without a limit and idle in [[src/core/utilities/messaging/rpc.spec.ts]].
