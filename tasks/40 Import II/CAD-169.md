Meshes above 100,000 triangles become solids after being simplified (decimated) to a chosen size, and open or broken meshes are repaired first.

- [x] Decimation (quadric error) to a triangle budget, with a preview
- [x] Repair: close holes, fix normals, remove duplicates
- [x] Then the existing mesh-to-solid
- [x] Test: a 500,000-triangle scan becomes a solid

Notes: decimation is Forstmann's threshold form of quadric error with a link-condition check ([[src/core/engines/interchange/decimate.ts]]); repair merges corners, drops repeated and collapsed triangles, turns triangles consistently and outwards, and closes openings with fans ([[src/core/engines/interchange/mesh-repair.ts]]) before every mesh-to-solid. The imported viewer's **Simplify** previews the result and keeps it as a new file "<name> (simplified)"; `import_file` takes `triangles` for agents. A 500,000-triangle scan simplifies in under 2 s and becomes a solid ([[src/core/engines/interchange/interchange-engine.spec.ts]]); journey in [[e2e/import-2.spec.ts]].
