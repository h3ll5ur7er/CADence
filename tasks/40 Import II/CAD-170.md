Import a KiCad board (`.kicad_pcb`): its outline, holes, mounting holes, and component outlines with their heights, to design an enclosure around it.

- [x] Read the board outline (Edge.Cuts), holes and pads
- [x] Components as boxes of their courtyard and height (or their STEP models when there)
- [x] An imported board as an assembly instance with named holes for joints
- [x] Test: a board imported, an enclosure positioned on its mounting holes

Notes: a small S-expression reader skips tracks, vias and zones ([[src/core/engines/interchange/kicad.ts]]); the board is one face with every drilled pad and cut-out as a hole, extruded by its thickness, its hole walls named `hole:<reference>` (`hole:<reference>:<pad>` for parts with several), and the parts one compound ([[src/core/engines/interchange/board.ts]]). Heights come from a footprint's `Height` field (else 3 mm); a part is its STEP model when the project holds a STEP file named like the model. The Modeling Engine keeps the names a file gives its faces when it inserts it. Tried on a real 11 MB keyboard board (420 footprints, 480 holes, 88 cut-outs): read in 0.1 s, built in 2 s, joints on its mounting holes work. Tests: [[src/core/engines/interchange/kicad.spec.ts]], the enclosure on H3 in [[src/core/managers/assembly/assembly-manager.spec.ts]], journey in [[e2e/import-2.spec.ts]].
