Compare two versions of a component side by side or overlaid in the viewport: what was added, removed and changed, in the timeline and in the bodies.

- [x] Pick two versions from the history
- [x] Overlay (added green, removed red) or side by side
- [x] Timeline and parameter differences listed
- [x] MCP: compare versions in words
- [x] Test: two versions of a plate with a new hole

Built ([[Architecture/Decisions#ADR-61]]): the Revision Engine lists what changed by items (timeline, sketch elements, parameters by name, settings); the Project Manager's `compareVersions` reads both versions, the Design Manager's `compareBodies` builds both and the Modeling Engine's `compare` gives the added and removed material. In the app: tick versions in the history, **Compare**; the compare view replaces the editor (overlaid or side by side, with the list and volumes). Agents: `versions` with `compare`. Tests: [[src/core/engines/revision/revision.spec.ts]], the plate with a new hole in the Modeling Engine's and Design Manager's specs, the journey in [[e2e/versions-and-collaboration.spec.ts]].
