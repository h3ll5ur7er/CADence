Open, view, measure and share models on a phone: a touch interface for viewing (not modelling), and shared links that open there.

- [x] A viewer layout for small screens: orbit, pan, zoom by touch
- [x] Measure by tapping
- [x] Open shared HTML viewers and projects on phones
- [x] Test: the viewer at phone size with touch

Notes ([[Architecture/Decisions#ADR-62]]):
- Below 720 px wide (or 500 px high on touch screens) the editor is a viewer (`Layout`, [[src/app/shared/layout.ts]]): the modes leave out the modelling tools, the browser and the properties panel and show `PhoneBar` ([[src/app/editor/phone-bar.ts]]): Documents (a drawer), Fit, Home, Measure, AR. Turning, panning and pinching are three.js's OrbitControls; a two-finger gesture no longer picks when the fingers lift.
- Measuring opens the measure panel as a sheet over the bottom of the model, in components and assemblies.
- The shared HTML viewer follows every pointer: one finger turns, two pan and pinch, a tap measures; its panel sits at the bottom on small screens with the parts folded away.
- Projects reach a phone as `.cadence` files, through git or as samples; "shared links" that open a project elsewhere need users and a server ([[CAD-177]], phase 42), so they are not part of this task.
- Journeys: `e2e/anywhere.spec.ts` (phone viewport, touch through the browser's own input), evidence `phase-43-phone-*.png`.
