- [x] glTF and USDZ export

Notes ([[Architecture/Decisions#ADR-62]]):
- `glb` and `usdz` in [[src/core/utilities/scene/index.ts]]: metres, Y up, a mesh and material per part; USDZ data aligned to 64 bytes (`zip(files, { align: 64 })`). Design and Assembly sessions: `viewerFile(format)`, `exportViewer(format)`; Export menu entries; MCP `export_component` and `export_assembly` take `glb` and `usdz`.
- AR ([[src/app/viewport/augmented-reality.ts]]): Quick Look through `<a rel="ar">` with the USDZ in Safari; WebXR `immersive-ar` with hit testing elsewhere ([[src/app/viewport/ar-session.ts]], a WebGL renderer loaded only then). The AR button shows only where one of them is found.
- Tests: the files read back with three.js's GLTFLoader and USDLoader ([[src/app/viewport/scene-files.spec.ts]]); the journeys stand in for Safari's link and for `navigator.xr` and check what the app asks of them. Placing a model in a real room is checked by hand on a device.
