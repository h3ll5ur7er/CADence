Reported by an agent modelling a keyboard enclosure through the MCP server (`cadence.exe mcp`, 2026-10-19/20, projects *KB enclosure* and *KB enclosure simple*), report 7.

After a fillet on the top shell's outer edges, or a cut far from the switch plate, `newFaces`/`goneFaces` list dozens of unrelated faces (switch cut-outs, the keycap opening, PCB bosses) as gone and new. They are still there; only their piece numbers (`~1`, `~2`) changed.

- [x] Faces whose name only changed its piece number, at the same place, are reported as renumbered, not as gone and new
- [x] Test: an edit that renumbers pieces reports no new or gone faces for them

Done in phase 33: `faceChanges` matches pieces by base name and centre; answers list them as `renumbered`.
