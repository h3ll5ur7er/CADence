Reported by an agent modelling a keyboard enclosure through the MCP server (`cadence.exe mcp`, 2026-10-19/20, projects *KB enclosure* and *KB enclosure simple*), report 11.

Spur gears (module 1, width 6, bore 5.1, 12 or 16 teeth) fail the print check with *walls down to 0.008 mm* (12 teeth) and *0.014 mm* (16) at points on the end faces in the middle of a tooth flank (r = 5.7 of 7). The helical gear with the same bore passes.

- [x] Find why the rays measure nearly nothing there (the faceted flanks next to the end face)
- [x] Gears that are fine pass
- [x] Test: a spur gear of 16 teeth passes the wall check

Done in phase 33: sliver triangles of the end faces along a flank cast their rays almost in the flank's facets, which they hit a hundredth away. A ray now leaves only through a face it meets within 40° of square.
