`render_picture` (and `cadence picture`) shows an assembly only as it stands, from one of seven fixed views. An agent cannot check what it built when it matters most for assemblies: the exploded view, a moment of a motion study, or a part hidden behind others. Found while planning [[CAD-96]].

- [ ] Pictures of an exploded view (all steps, or the first n)
- [ ] Pictures at a time of a motion study, and a short animation (GIF or WebM) of a study
- [ ] A free camera: direction (and up), and zoom onto chosen instances or bodies
- [ ] Hide instances for the picture, or show them see-through
