`render_picture` (and `cadence picture`) shows an assembly only as it stands, from one of seven fixed views. An agent cannot check what it built when it matters most for assemblies: the exploded view, a moment of a motion study, or a part hidden behind others. Found while planning [[CAD-96]].

- [x] Pictures of an exploded view (all steps, or the first n)
- [x] Pictures at a time of a motion study, and a short animation (GIF or WebM) of a study
- [x] A free camera: direction (and up), and zoom onto chosen instances or bodies
- [x] Hide instances for the picture, or show them see-through

**Done (phase 26).** `render_picture` takes `explode` (and `steps`), `study` and `time`, a free `direction` and `up`, and `focus`, `hide` and `ghost` lists of body or instance ids; the command line's `picture` takes the same options. `animate_study` writes an animated GIF ([[src/core/utilities/picture/gif.ts]]) and returns a sheet of moments as one PNG, since agents look at still pictures.
