Joining a cylinder (an extruded circle) onto a revolved body, with the cylinder's axis crossing the revolve axis, is refused with "The result is not a valid solid" for some diameters and accepted for others close by. A circular pattern of the same pad fails for most diameters. Found in [[CAD-96]] (Crankcase: nine flat pads for the cylinder barrels on a revolved drum); the crankcase cannot be modelled as designed.

## Reproduce

A drum revolved about Z from the XZ outline `[24,60] [45,60] [45,36] [105,36] [105,-36] [45,-36] [45,-60] [24,-60] [24,-30] [78,-30] [78,30] [24,30]`; a plane offset 105 mm from YZ; on it a circle of diameter *d* at (0, 0), extruded 15 mm `reverse`, `join`; then a `circular-pattern` of that extrude about Z, 9 copies over 320°.

| d (mm) | 50 | 55 | 58 | 59 | 60 | 60.5 | 61 | 61.5 | 62 | 63 | 65 | 68 | 70 | 72 | 75 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| pad | ok | ok | fails | fails | ok | ok | fails | ok | fails | fails | fails | ok | ok | ok | ok |
| pattern | fails | fails | | | fails | fails | | fails | | | | fails | fails | ok | ok |

(The pattern was only tried where the pad itself works.) The sweep was run with `cadence run` scripts on a scratch component; the same failures happen through `edit_component`.

## Where to look

`store` rejects the result when `BRepCheck_Analyzer` says it is invalid ([[src/core/engines/modeling/occt-modeling-engine.ts#L402-L407]]). The booleans come from replicad's `fuseShapes`/`cutShape`, which call `SimplifyResult(true, true, 0.001)` (unify same domain) on every result; merging the faces of the pad's cylinder where it meets the drum's cylinder is a likely cause. Things to try: check validity before and after simplifying, fall back to the unsimplified result (or `ShapeFix_Shape`) when the simplified one is invalid, and fuse all pattern copies in one boolean (several tools) instead of one after the other.
