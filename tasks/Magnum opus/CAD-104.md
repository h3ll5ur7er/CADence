id: CAD-104
title: Faces of different sketch curves on one surface are named after one of them
type: bug
status: done
priority: medium
labels: [modeling, naming, mcp, found-by-CAD-96]
---

Generative naming ([[src/core/engines/modeling/naming.ts]]) gives a face the name of the first candidate on the same surface. When one feature makes several faces from different sketch curves that lie on one surface, they all take one curve's name and are told apart only by `~1`, `~2` in position order. The names are stable, but misleading for anyone (agents above all) who reads them to find a face:

- Crankshaft ([[CAD-96]]): one revolve of two outlines, `front` and `rear`; the front journal (z 25…60) is named `f:shafts:side:rear:l4~2`, after the rear outline.
- Crankcase, rear half: the rear bearing boss (outline `rb`) is named `f:boss:side:fb:l2` and `f:boss:side:fb:l4~1`, after the front boss `fb`, although the front boss is not in that body at all.
- Cylinder barrel: the eleven wall pieces between the fins come from eleven sketch lines (`l6`, `l10`, … `l50`) and are all `f:rev:side:prof:l6~1…12`.

Worse, the `~n` numbers are given by position, so a later feature renumbers faces it does not touch. Spark plug: the ring above the hex was `f:body:side:sp:l2~2`; after a modelled thread on the shank below it, the ring is `f:body:side:sp:l2~8` and `l2~2` names a thread crest. Any feature that referred to `l2~2` (a chamfer on the ring, a sketch on it) now silently refers to another face, with no error to repair.

Related, also seen: patterned new bodies are numbered `kpat`, `kpat:3` … `kpat:8` (no `kpat:2`), and copies of a face split in two (`kp~1`, `kp~2`) are all named after `kp~2` (`f:kh:side:kp~2@kpat.3~1`).

- [x] Within one feature, name a fresh face after the sketch curve it was swept from, not after another curve on the same surface
- [x] Keep the `~n` of faces a feature does not touch (or flag references whose face changed), so references do not jump
- [x] Number patterned bodies without gaps

**Done (phase 26).** Faces and edges a feature leaves alone keep their full names; others are numbered skipping kept numbers. Of earlier faces on one surface, a piece takes the name of the one it lies in; in a tool, each curve's sweep is recognised by its middle. Bodies are numbered without gaps (`kpat`, `kpat:2`, …), which renames patterned bodies of earlier documents. [[Architecture/Decisions#ADR-44]].

**Reopened (2026-10-16, checking phase 26 in [[CAD-96]]).** The fix holds for new bodies (the barrel's wall pieces are named after their own lines now, and a revolve of two outlines as a new body names the front faces `front:…`), but not when the revolve **joins** an existing body: the Crankshaft's front journal is still `f:shafts:side:rear:l4~2`. Minimal repro (in the project's *Scratch tests* component): an extruded disc ⌀60 mm from z 13 to 25 (new body), then a revolve about Z of two outlines on XZ, `front` `[0,13] [20,13] [20,60] [0,60]` and `rear` `[0,-60] [20,-60] [20,-13] [0,-13]`, operation `join`. The front cylinder (z 25…60) is named `f:shafts:side:rear:l2~2`; with operation `new-body` it is `f:shafts:side:front:l2`. The piece of a tool face that a join trims seems to lose the curve it was swept from.

- [x] Name the trimmed pieces of a joined tool's faces after their own sketch curve (test: the repro above, `join`)

**Done again (phase 27).** The cause: finding which earlier face a piece lies in took a point at the middle of the face's raw parameter range, but replicad takes 0 … 1 across it, so the point missed both tool faces and the first one won. It now takes a point inside the face (a few spots tried, so a hole in the face does not matter). Test: the repro above, as `join`.
