Small things that cost an agent time while modelling [[CAD-96]]; none blocked the work.

- [ ] **Rename and delete documents over MCP.** A new project's first component is called "Component 1" for good, and a component modelled wrongly cannot be removed; the Project Manager can do both, the MCP server has no tools for it.
- [ ] **The sketch origin as a placement point.** A part (and a hole) placed at `points: ["origin"]` is refused with "Some of its points no longer exist. Edit the part and pick its sketch again", which sends the agent looking for a deleted point; an extra point coincident with the origin works. Accept the origin, or say that it is not accepted.
- [ ] **Say which constraints conflict.** Two refusals named no constraint: "This conflicts with existing constraints (). Remove one of them first." (a point held on a line tangent to an arc's circle, which is singular, not contradictory), and "conflicts with existing constraints (fixed point)" for an arc whose centre and both ends are fixed at consistent positions (redundant, not contradictory). List the constraints involved, and tell redundant from contradictory.
- [ ] **Positions as expressions in the shorthands.** `add-circle`, `add-rectangle` and `add-polygon` take numbers for positions, so a hole at `rod` mm from the origin cannot follow the parameter: the master rod's small end stays at 110 mm when `rod` changes. Accept expressions for `x` and `y` (and points), dimensioned from the origin.
