title: Pictures and animations of large assemblies crash with "Maximum call stack size exceeded"
type: bug
status: done
priority: high
labels: [rendering, mcp, automation, found-by-CAD-96]
---

`rasterize` ([[src/core/utilities/raster/index.ts#L100]]) frames the picture with `Math.min(...all.map(…))` and `Math.max(...)` over every vertex of every shown mesh. Spreading an array that large into a call exceeds the engine's argument limit, so `render_picture`, `animate_study` and `cadence picture` fail with `RangeError: Maximum call stack size exceeded` once an assembly has enough vertices. Found in [[CAD-96]]: the radial engine (71 instances, 50 pattern copies) rendered fine with 23 instances and fails with all of them, whatever the view, exploded or not.

Checked again after a fresh start of the app (2026-10-16): the plain picture still fails within seconds, while pictures with `focus` on a few instances work, also at a moment of the study. Only the focused meshes go into the bounds, which confirms the cause.

This blocks every picture an agent can take of the finished engine, and the animation of its motion study ([[CAD-100]]).

- [x] Compute the bounds in a loop (or with `reduce`), never by spreading vertex arrays; check the other spreads over mesh data (`instructions.ts`, the viewer export, build-instruction pictures)
- [x] Test: a picture of an assembly with a few million vertices

**Done (phase 27).** `rasterize` bounds its points in a loop; so do imported drawings' extents and sketch exports (a `range` helper in the math utilities). Other spreads go over a few values only. Test: a picture of a mesh of a million vertices. The whole radial engine renders in 0.7 s.
