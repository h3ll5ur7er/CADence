id: CAD-113
title: Joint offsets cannot tilt a joint frame
type: feature
status: done
priority: low
labels: [assemblies, joints, found-by-CAD-96]
---

A joint offset moves the frame along its axes and turns it about its normal, but cannot tilt it. A part that must follow another along a direction no face of either part has cannot be joined. In [[CAD-96]] the pushrods run from the tappets (which slide radially, driven by cam links) to the rocker boxes at 21° to the cylinder axis; with no face along that direction they cannot be jointed to the tappets and stay grounded, so the valve train stops at the tappets.

- [x] An offset with a full rotation (or a tilt about the frame's u axis), or a joint origin given as a frame in component coordinates

**Done (phase 27)**, as a tilt: `offset.tilt` (degrees) turns the frame about its own x axis after moving it and before the turn about its normal. The joint panel has a *Tilt* field.
