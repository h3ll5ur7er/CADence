`check_assembly` with a study (the *Running at 60 rpm* study of the radial engine in [[CAD-96]]: 4 s at 4 moments a second, 16 moments; 71 instances and 50 pattern copies) gave no answer and no progress for 30 minutes, and Claude Code aborted it ("sent no response or progress for 1800s"). The static interference check of the same assembly answers in under a minute. An agent cannot check its mechanism for collisions, and the server process stays busy meanwhile.

- [x] Profile the study check on a large assembly; only test pairs whose relative pose changed since the last moment, and pairs whose bounding boxes overlap
- [x] Send MCP progress notifications during long checks (moment n of m), and offer a time limit
- [x] Test: the radial engine's study checks in well under the client's idle timeout

**Done (phase 27).** A pair is tested again only when it moved against itself; pairs are screened by their boxes (measured once per body) and their meshes (a bounding volume hierarchy, separating axes, ray parity for one inside the other) before the kernel's boolean, and the boolean no longer leaks clones (the check ran out of kernel memory after a dozen moments). `check_assembly` sends progress notifications and takes `seconds`. The kernel's boolean itself grows its memory by about 180 KB a call (in the WebAssembly build), so surfaces that only touch are told from overlapping ones by rays through the meshes and never reach it; replicad's `cast` and `Transformation` left handles and builders behind, now freed (`adopt`). The radial engine's 16 s study at 4 moments a second (65 moments, 121 parts) checks in 7 minutes at a steady 1.1 GB, against out of memory after 20 moments before; it finds 55 collisions, the design mistakes listed in [[Magnum opus#Status]].
