The propeller of the radial engine is one blade on the turning hub and a circular pattern of it (`pat_blades`, 3 × 120°). At rest all three blades sit on the hub. In the motion study only the original blade turns with the hub; the two copies stay where they were at rest, cutting through the turning blade and hub. It shows in the app's playback and in `render_picture` with `study` and `time` (front view, focus on the hub and blades, at 0 s and at 0.5 s: blade 1 has turned 51°, the copies have not moved). Found in [[CAD-96]].

The cause: `motion()` returns the frames of `kinematics.simulate()`, which hold poses for the document's instances only ([[src/core/managers/assembly/default-assembly-manager.ts#L176-L180]]). Copies get their pose only in the static derivation, from the original's pose at rest ([[src/core/managers/assembly/default-assembly-manager.ts#L543]]). The app keeps the rest pose for an instance without a preview pose, so copies stand still. The collision check of a study leaves copies out entirely ([[src/core/managers/assembly/default-assembly-manager.ts#L213]]), so it can miss collisions with them, for example the blades and the static pushrods against the moving tappets in the engine.

- [x] Each motion frame carries the copies' poses, the pattern's transform composed with the original's pose in that frame (joint previews already do, through `derive()`)
- [x] The collision check of a study includes pattern copies
- [x] A test: a pattern of an instance on a revolute joint turns with it in a study

**Done (phase 27).** Motion frames carry the copies' poses (the pattern's transform after the original's pose in that frame), and the study's collision check includes the copies. Test: a copy of an instance on a revolute joint turns with it.
