Animating the radial engine of [[CAD-96]] (round 3) with `animate_study`:

- **The camera jumps.** Each frame is drawn on its own ([[src/automation/api.ts#L687-L699]]: `pixels()` per frame), so the view is fitted again to that frame's parts. With `focus` on the pistons the engine moves up and down by about a tenth of the picture between frames while the pistons travel. The view should be fitted once, to the parts over the whole study (or to the first frame), and kept.
- **The sheet can show one pose nine times.** Its moments are spread evenly over the study (0, 2, 4, … 16 s); a study of whole turns at 60 rpm then shows crank angle 0 in every picture. Moments that avoid whole periods of the driven joints, or a note in the answer, would help.
- **The GIF lands in the server's working directory.** Without `path`, the MCP server (started without `--exports`) wrote `Running at 60 rpm.gif` into the repository it was started in. The default could be the project's own folder or a temporary one, and the answer could say where it is going before the long render.

- [ ] One camera for all frames of an animation (fitted to the study's whole motion)
- [ ] Sheet moments that differ for periodic studies (or say why they look alike)
- [ ] A default exports folder outside the working directory, or the tool description says where it is
