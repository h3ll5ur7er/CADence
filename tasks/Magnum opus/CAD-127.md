Dimensioning 27 part drawings and one assembly drawing of the radial engine ([[CAD-96]], round 5) worked in the end, but each of these cost time or forced a note instead of a dimension:

- **Outlines of round faces cannot be dimensioned.** Anchors are model vertices and edges ([[src/core/contracts/drawing.ts#L57-L61]]). On a revolved part, a cylinder's outline in a side view is a silhouette, not an edge, so the visible outline offers nothing to pick. What is offered are the revolve's seam edges and vertices, which project onto the centre line. Lengths therefore run from points on the axis, and diameters only exist as circles in an end view (no ⌀ on a side view, the usual way to dimension a shaft).
- **No dimension between circle centres.** Linear dimensions take two points. The master rod's crankpin-to-wrist-pin distance (110) and the link rod's (76) cannot be dimensioned: the seam vertices on the two bores give 122 or 98. They became notes.
- **Diameter and radius dimensions cannot be placed.** Every one leans 45° up and right; `offset` only sets how far the text sits outside the circle. Concentric diameters (piston: ⌀49.8, ⌀46.8, ⌀40) stack on one leader. Their texts collide unless the circle radius plus the offset differs by about 8 mm, and near a sheet edge they run off the frame. The crankshaft's R62 and ⌀40 could not both be placed.
- **Hidden geometry is offered as an anchor.** With hidden lines off, `get_drawing` still lists circles that are not drawn, without saying so. The planet carrier's ⌀12 pin holes on the lower plate sit right under the ⌀8 hub bolt holes. A dimension landed on the invisible one and pointed at the wrong hole.
- **A section has no cutting-plane line.** *Section A-A* is cut through the middle of the model, but no view shows where (a chain line with arrows and the letters).
- **Assembly views have no anchors.** `anchorCounts` is empty for views of an assembly, so an assembly drawing cannot carry overall dimensions (height, width, propeller diameter).
- **The title block's material is not the part's.** It is typed by hand. The magneto has no material set, so its parts-list row shows the default *PLA* while the title block showed what was typed.

- [ ] Silhouette lines of round faces as anchors (and their ends as points), so outlines can be dimensioned; a ⌀ dimension between two silhouettes of one cylinder
- [ ] Dimensions from and to a circle's centre (centre distance, centre to edge)
- [ ] Diameter and radius dimensions with an angle (or a text position), and a leader that stays on the sheet
- [ ] Anchors say whether they are visible in the view; hidden ones are not offered unless hidden lines are drawn
- [ ] Section views mark their cutting plane on the view they are taken from
- [ ] Anchors on assembly views (instance vertices and edges, or at least the views' extents) for overall dimensions
- [ ] The title block's material follows the drawn component's (editable)
