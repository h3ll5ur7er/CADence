Checking [[CAD-127]] on the radial engine's planet carrier ([[CAD-96]], top view, hidden lines off): two of the three ⌀12 planet-pin circles on the lower plate are no longer offered as anchors, as they should not be, but the third one (`c1mzjeji`, model point (−21, 36.4, −1)) still is, without `hidden`. It sits 2 mm off the centre of a ⌀8 bolt hole in the top flange (model z 140). Its circle stays outside the hole except at one point where it touches the hole's edge, and the view draws none of it. A dimension on it would point at the bolt hole, the mistake [[CAD-127]] was about.

Probably the visibility test samples the circle and counts the touching point as seen.

- [x] A circle counts as seen only where a stretch of it is drawn (not at a single tangent point)
- [x] Test: a circle below a smaller, slightly offset hole is hidden in the view from above

Fixed in phase 30 ([[Architecture/Decisions#ADR-49]]): a line or circle counts as seen only where two neighbouring samples along it lie on what the view draws (circles sampled 48 times a turn, lines 21 times), so one that only touches a drawn edge at a single point is hidden; points still count where they are drawn. The cached views' stamp changed (`views:3`), so drawings made before are drawn again once. Test: [[src/core/engines/modeling/drawing-view.spec.ts]] (a ⌀12 pocket under the plate's ⌀10 hole, touching it at a sampled angle, failed before the fix).
