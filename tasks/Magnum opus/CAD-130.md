Since the time limit of [[CAD-125]], views whose exact projection takes more than 20 s are drawn from the triangles. On the radial engine's assembly drawing ([[CAD-96]], R9-00, A2 at 1:5) all three views now take that path (note *Drawn from the triangles: the exact projection took too long.*). They look right, but they consist of 108,262 (front), 57,292 (side) and 65,766 (iso) paths of two points each. The exported PDF grew from 6.3 MB to 12.2 MB; the part drawings did not change.

- [x] Join the segments of one edge or outline into polylines (and merge collinear runs), so a mesh-drawn view is about as large as an exact one
- [ ] For assembly views, maybe a longer limit or the exact projection finishing in the background and replacing the mesh view once done (kept in the drawing's result)
- [x] Test: a mesh-drawn view of a cylinder has a handful of paths, not one per triangle edge

Fixed in phase 30 ([[Architecture/Decisions#ADR-49]]): runs of samples seen alike become lines between their corners (Douglas–Peucker at half a pixel, also for circles seen edge on, which come as 24 lines), outlines from the triangles are chained along shared corners, and the sheet joins lines that go on where the one before ends into one path. On a copy of the engine, the assembly's front view drawn from the triangles is 3,657 paths (108,262 reported before) and its sheet's PDF 220 KB; the spark plug's top view 18 paths. Not done: a longer limit or finishing the exact projection in the background for assembly views; with this few paths the triangle views are as small as exact ones, and the views worker would stay busy for minutes.
