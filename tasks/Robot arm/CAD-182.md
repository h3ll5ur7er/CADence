A six-axis robot arm modelled through the MCP server as a stress test, like the radial engine ([[CAD-96]]): a chain of revolute joints with limits, a gripper, cable routes. *Stresses:* long kinematic chains, joint limits, motion studies, collision checks over the reach, the strength check of the links. Each finding is filed as a task in this epic.

**Status (2026-10-21, round 1): the arm is modelled, moves and is drawn; eleven findings are filed.** The project *Robot arm R6* has eleven components, three standard parts and the assembly *Robot arm*: 24 instances and 3 pattern copies, 6 revolute joints with limits, 2 linked finger sliders and 13 rigid joints for motors, bearings and a screw. It has two motion studies (*Reach*, *Pick and place*), an animation, the exploded view *Taken apart*, the upper arm's strength check and nine drawings (R6-00 to R6-08). Standing upright nothing overlaps; *Reach* (501 moments) is free of collisions; *Pick and place* hits once, because the gripper cannot carry the workpiece ([[CAD-187]]). The design and the findings are in [[Robot arm]].
