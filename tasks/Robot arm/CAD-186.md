The robot arm's shoulder has limits of −90° … 90° ([[CAD-182]]). `set-joint-values` with 120 set 90; only the value echoed in the answer showed it. A motion study keyed to 150° and a drive that turns the flange 900° were added, played and checked for collisions without a word. The study holds the shoulder at 90° from 2.4 s on, and the picture at 4 s shows it there. The kinematic solver clamps ([[src/core/engines/kinematics/kinematic-solver.ts#L53]]), and nothing says so.

The Stewart platform ([[CAD-183]]) shows the same in a closed mechanism. A study keyed to lift the plate 72 mm asks 120.5 mm of each leg, and it stops at the stroke's 100 mm from 14.3 s on. A limit of 10° on one base ball below its home tilt of 13.2° is kept, and the other five legs move the plate 13 mm to close the loops. Neither says so.

An agent planning a pick-and-place cycle needs to know when a pose it asked for cannot be reached: the gripper is somewhere else than it planned.

- [ ] `set-joint-values` outside a limit says which joint was held at which limit (the value is still set to the limit)
- [ ] Adding or changing a study whose keys or drives go past a limit says which joints and from when
- [ ] check_assembly with a study also reports the moments a joint runs into its limit, beside the collisions
- [ ] The app marks keys past a limit in the study's timeline
