The robot arm's *Pick and place* study ([[CAD-182]], [[Robot arm]]) closes the gripper on a 30 mm cube, lifts it and puts it down elsewhere. The cube cannot come along. It is grounded on the table, and an instance belongs to one joint for the whole study. So the gripper lifts off without it, and check_assembly reports finger B hitting the cube at 3.6 s as the fingers leave. Moving the cube with joints keyed to follow the gripper would be a workaround; the study needs to say what really happens.

What it takes: from a moment of the study on, an instance is held by another one (the gripper), keeping where it was relative to it, and from a later moment it is let go where it is then (on the table, at the place spot). The same serves conveyors, a tool change, parts handed from one robot to another and assembly sequences.

- [x] A study can attach an instance to another instance at a time and detach it at a later time (rigidly, where it is at that moment)
- [x] Pictures, animations, collision checks and the app's playback show the carried part moving
- [x] Collision checks leave out the carrier and the carried part while they are attached (they touch on purpose)
- [x] MCP: `add-study` takes the attachments; get_assembly lists them
- [x] Check: the robot arm's *Pick and place* moves the cube from the pick spot to the place spot without collisions

Fixed in phase 45 ([[Architecture/Decisions#ADR-64]]): a study's `carries` (instance, carrier, from, until) keep a part on its carrier from a moment on and leave it where it was at the end. Pictures, animations, playback and moments follow; collision checks leave a part and its carrier out while it is carried. The motion panel has *Carried parts*. Only parts no joint holds can be carried. Checked on the robot arm: the cube carried by finger A from 3.5 s until 8 s (when the fingers open) travels from the pick spot to the place spot and lands at (−150.009, −250.006, 0.088), with no collision over 241 moments.
