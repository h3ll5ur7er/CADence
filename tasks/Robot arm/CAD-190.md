The robot arm's upper arm ([[CAD-182]]) was checked held at its ⌀12 shoulder axles, with 21.5 N on the elbow axle (everything after the elbow and a 1 kg payload). With the default element size, the answer looked safe and settled; finer meshes changed it by a factor of four:

| Element size | Elements | Largest stress | Deflection | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| default (≈ 11.8 mm) | 1,350 | 2.75 MPa | 0.0385 mm | 2 s |
| 5 mm | 28,908 | 3.95 MPa | 0.0138 mm | 8 s |
| 3 mm | 124,464 | 8.36 MPa | 0.0195 mm | 37 s |
| 2.5 mm | 215,004 | 12.66 MPa | 0.0182 mm | 68 s |

By hand, the axles carry the 4.7 N·m about the shoulder in torsion: about 7 MPa shear in each, 12 MPa von Mises. The default size aims at about 800 cubes ([[src/core/engines/analysis/fea.ts#L252-L254]]), so the ⌀12 axles that hold the part are about one element across. The stress also keeps rising at the axles' sharp roots, where it has no finite value.

- [x] The default size takes the smallest held and loaded faces into account (several elements across each), not only the part's box
- [x] The answer says how settled it is: the result at the next coarser size beside it, or a warning when they differ by more than 10 %
- [x] A peak at a sharp inside corner is reported as such (with the stress a little away from it), with a hint to round the corner
- [x] Open point *mesh that refines where stresses peak* ([[Open points]]) is the larger version of this

Fixed in phase 46 ([[Architecture/Decisions#ADR-65]]): the default element size gives at least three elements across the smallest held or loaded face. Every check runs again with elements half as large again and reports `coarser`, `settled` (within 10 %) and a note when not. A peak more than 1.5 times the stress two elements away is noted as a small spot (a sharp corner), with the stress away from it and a hint to round the corner. The panel shows the element size, the coarser stress and the notes. On the upper arm: the default is now 3.67 mm (66,732 elements, 20 s) and reads 9.03 MPa instead of 2.75. It says it is not settled (4.37 MPa at 5.5 mm, 52 % off), as the axles' sharp roots never settle.
