The robot arm ([[CAD-182]]) needs its motor cables run from the base through every link to the wrist. Inside one link this works: a sweep of a ⌀6 circle along a line and a spline in the upper arm's pocket. Beyond that:

- **3D paths.** A sweep follows the curves of one sketch, so a path lies in one plane. A cable that leaves the pocket, turns round the elbow boss and enters the next housing needs a path in space: through points on several planes, or along edges.
- **Across joints.** A cable from the upper arm into the elbow housing changes shape with the elbow angle. A component cannot do that, and nothing in an assembly bends. So no cable can be checked for its length, its bend radius or for hitting the arm over the reach.

- [x] Sweep paths in space: a 3D polyline or spline through points (and points on other sketches or edges), with tangent ends
- [x] An assembly cable or hose: a diameter and points on instances it runs through (with the direction it leaves each), shaped smoothly between them in every pose and in motion studies
- [x] Its length in each pose, the longest it gets over a study, and where it bends tighter than its minimum radius
- [x] Collision checks include cables (against the parts they do not pass through)
- [x] It appears in the bill of materials with its length

Fixed in phase 46 ([[Architecture/Decisions#ADR-65]]): a `path` feature (*Path in space*) is lines between points or a smooth curve through them, with tangents at the ends; points are expressions or points of sketches (not yet points on edges). Sweeps take it as a path. Assemblies have `cables`: a diameter and points of instances with the directions they leave, shaped by a smooth curve in every pose and frame, drawn as tubes in the app and in pictures, and listed in the browser and the bill of materials with their length. check_assembly with a study reports where a cable runs through a part, and its longest and tightest moments. Tried on the robot arm with a cable from the turret along the upper arm into the elbow housing: the check found the parts the hastily chosen route runs through, and a 1.15 mm bend when the elbow folds. A cable routed properly is for round 2.
