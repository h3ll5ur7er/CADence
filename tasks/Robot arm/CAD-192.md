The robot arm ([[CAD-182]]) needs its motor cables run from the base through every link to the wrist. Inside one link this works: a sweep of a ⌀6 circle along a line and a spline in the upper arm's pocket. Beyond that:

- **3D paths.** A sweep follows the curves of one sketch, so a path lies in one plane. A cable that leaves the pocket, turns round the elbow boss and enters the next housing needs a path in space: through points on several planes, or along edges.
- **Across joints.** A cable from the upper arm into the elbow housing changes shape with the elbow angle. A component cannot do that, and nothing in an assembly bends. So no cable can be checked for its length, its bend radius or for hitting the arm over the reach.

- [ ] Sweep paths in space: a 3D polyline or spline through points (and points on other sketches or edges), with tangent ends
- [ ] An assembly cable or hose: a diameter and points on instances it runs through (with the direction it leaves each), shaped smoothly between them in every pose and in motion studies
- [ ] Its length in each pose, the longest it gets over a study, and where it bends tighter than its minimum radius
- [ ] Collision checks include cables (against the parts they do not pass through)
- [ ] It appears in the bill of materials with its length
