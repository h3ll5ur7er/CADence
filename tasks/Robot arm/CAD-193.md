The robot arm's drawings ([[CAD-182]]) are R6-00 to R6-08, nine sheets in all. Three things showed:

- **Overflow.** The parts list and the title block keep their column widths whatever the text. In R6-00, *Stepper motor (average)* and *Aluminium, Cable bundle* run out of the material column and past the sheet's frame. R6-03's title block cut *Aluminium, Cable bundl…* off at the sheet edge before its material was set by hand.
- **Unknown size.** The parts list is placed by its top right corner, but nothing says how big it gets before it is drawn. On R6-00 it covered the side view until a picture showed it.
- **No leaving out.** A view shows every body of a component and every instance of an assembly. R6-03 draws the cable swept through the upper arm's pocket. R6-00 draws the 650 mm table, which sets the sheet's scale to 1:5 for an arm 150 mm wide.

- [ ] Parts list columns and title block cells fit their texts (wider columns, or smaller or wrapped text), never past the frame
- [ ] get_drawing reports each annotation's box (the parts list's above all), and the edit answers with it
- [ ] Views can leave out bodies (of a component) and instances (of an assembly)
