The robot arm ([[CAD-182]]) takes its bearings from the standard parts: four 6201 (12 × 32 × 10) for the waist, shoulder and elbow and two 608 for the wrist. The library's largest bearing is the 6201 ([[src/core/contracts/parts.ts]], `BearingSize`). Robot joints carry their loads on large, thin bearings around a hollow shaft for the cables: 6805 (25 × 37 × 7), 6806, 61808, crossed roller bearings. The motors are drawn by hand, as one component with three configurations (NEMA 17, 11 and 8). Every arm, printer and CNC machine needs them.

- [ ] Ball bearings 6002 … 6005, 6202 … 6205 and the thin series 6800 … 6810, 61800 … 61810
- [ ] Stepper motors NEMA 8, 11, 14, 17 and 23 in their common lengths, with the shaft, boss and screw holes
- [ ] `insert_library_part` lays them on a hole or a face as it does the bearings today
