A Stewart platform modelled through the MCP server as a stress test: a base and a top plate joined by six legs, each an actuator (a slider) between two ball or universal joints. Simple parts, hard motion: six closed loops share one plate. *Stresses:* many coupled loops solved at once, driving the plate's pose and getting the leg lengths (no joint gives that today), joint angle limits and leg collisions over the reach, universal joints (only ball joints exist), motion studies of the plate tilting and turning. Each finding is filed as a task in this epic.

**Status (2026-10-21, round 1): modelled and moving; four findings filed.** The project *Stewart platform S6* has four components and the assembly *Stewart platform*: 14 instances and 18 joints (six base balls, six leg sliders, six top balls) in six closed loops. Its studies are *Tilt, turn and lift* and *Reach*, and nothing overlaps at home or while they play. Driving the legs one after another moves the others ([[CAD-195]]); a study that keys all six holds them. The design, the checks and the findings are in [[Stewart platform]].
