The Stewart platform's study *Tilt, turn and lift* ([[CAD-183]]) keys the six legs for a 10° roll, a 10° pitch, a 20° turn, a 40 mm lift and a 40 mm shift. Whether the plate really gets there can only be seen: render_picture shows a moment of a study, but nothing gives its numbers. get_assembly answers with the assembly as it stands, so checking a study means comparing pictures by eye. The robot arm's pick-and-place study ([[CAD-182]]) has the same gap: where are the fingertips at 3 s?

- [x] get_assembly (or a tool of its own) answers for a study and a time: every instance's transform and every joint's values then
- [x] And for a list of times, or every frame at a rate, kept short (only chosen instances or joints)
- [x] Measuring tools take a study moment as well (a distance between two instances' points at 4 s)

Fixed in phase 45 ([[Architecture/Decisions#ADR-64]]): frames carry every joint's values. `at(study, time)` plays the study up to that moment (about 50 frames a second, the last one exactly then). `get_assembly` with `study` and `time` (or up to 50 `times`, only the `instances` and `joints` asked for) answers with every instance's transform and origin and every joint's values then. `measure` takes a study and a time.
