The Stewart platform's study *Tilt, turn and lift* ([[CAD-183]]) keys the six legs for a 10° roll, a 10° pitch, a 20° turn, a 40 mm lift and a 40 mm shift. Whether the plate really gets there can only be seen: render_picture shows a moment of a study, but nothing gives its numbers. get_assembly answers with the assembly as it stands, so checking a study means comparing pictures by eye. The robot arm's pick-and-place study ([[CAD-182]]) has the same gap: where are the fingertips at 3 s?

- [ ] get_assembly (or a tool of its own) answers for a study and a time: every instance's transform and every joint's values then
- [ ] And for a list of times, or every frame at a rate, kept short (only chosen instances or joints)
- [ ] Measuring tools take a study moment as well (a distance between two instances' points at 4 s)
